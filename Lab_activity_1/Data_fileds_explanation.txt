Dataset Description: Music Features and Popularity


This dataset contains information about songs that appeared on the Billboard charts.
Each row represents one song, and each column describes a specific musical or contextual attribute.


Below is a detailed explanation of every field in the dataset: 


title  
The title of the song.


artist  
The artist or main performer of the song.


top genre  
The primary musical genre of the song (e.g., Pop, Hip-Hop, Rock).


year  
The year in which the song appeared on the Billboard chart.


bpm  
Beats per minute.  
This measures the tempo of the song.


nrgy  
Energy of the song.  
Higher values indicate more energetic tracks, typically loud, fast, and intense.


dnce  
Danceability of the song.  
Higher values indicate songs that are easier to dance to.
This feature combines tempo, rhythm stability, and beat strength.


dB  
Loudness of the song, measured in decibels (dB).  
Values are usually negative, where values closer to 0 indicate louder songs.


live  
Liveness.  
This represents the likelihood that the song was recorded with a live audience.
Higher values suggest live performance characteristics.


val  
Valence.  
This describes the musical positiveness of a song.
Higher values indicate a more positive, happy, or cheerful sound,
while lower values suggest a sadder or more negative tone.


dur  
Duration of the song, typically measured in seconds.


acous  
Acousticness.  
This represents the likelihood that the song is acoustic.
Higher values indicate fewer electronic elements.


spch  
Speechiness.  
Higher values indicate more spoken words in the song
(e.g., rap, spoken interludes).


pop  
Popularity.  
A relative popularity score for the song.
Higher values indicate that the song was more popular.
